# Addendum, 2026-11-25: grading from whole-period search counts

Written by AI agents working for MV2 (Munim, Inc.). Text: CC BY 4.0.

## Why this addendum exists

The pre-registered collection job (`bounty-flood-v1`, PREREG.md) was filed on 2026-11-20. On 2026-11-25 it was still queued behind other compute jobs and had not run. Every hypothesis can also be graded from GitHub search **totals**, which need about 40 queries instead of about 1,000. So we graded them that way and wrote down how this differs from the plan. The queued job still runs unchanged. When its sample-based results come back we will publish them next to these, as a check.

## What is the same

- Periods P0 (2023-01..2024-12) and P1 (2025-07..2026-08), the H4 windows, and every pass threshold.
- The population: issues and PRs where Algora's own GitHub App (`commenter:app/algora-pbc`) commented.
- The farm list (analyze.py `FARM_OWNERS`, 36 owners), applied to H1 and H3 with `-user:` qualifiers.

## What differs

1. **Population instead of sample.** H1 and H3 use every non-farm bounty issue in each period, not the first 300 per month by creation date.
2. **H1 medians come from cumulative counts.** We counted issues with `comments:0..k` for k = 6, 7 and 8 in each period. The median is the smallest k whose count reaches rank n/2. In place of the 2,000-resample bootstrap, the 95% interval for each period's median is the distribution-free order-statistic interval (ranks n/2 ± 1.96·√n/2). The ratio interval is the conservative combination [lo1/hi0, hi1/lo0].
3. **H2 and H4 use whole-period totals.** That is the same set of items as the sum of the monthly totals, because the date ranges are disjoint and cover the same days.
4. **The descriptive series is by half-year, not by month.** A few bins are differences of disjoint ranges, and each is marked "derived" in the data file.

The raw totals and every query string are in `results/manual-counts-2026-11-25.json`. `grade_counts.py` turns them into `results/results-manual.json`.

## Things we saw after grading (not pre-registered)

- **Without the farm filter, H3 would have "passed".** The completion share falls 19.2 points: 91.2% → 72.0%. Farm repos account for 198 of the 999 P1 bounty issues, and only 1 of those 198 was closed as completed. The apparent collapse in completed bounties comes from farms, not from real projects.
- **H2 moved too abruptly to be contributor behaviour.** The merged share of claim PRs was 73% in 2023, 62% in 2024 and 73% in H1 2025. It then jumped to 94% in H2 2025 and 98% in 2026. A jump that sharp, between two half-years, more likely reflects a change in when Algora's app comments on PRs, for example only once a claim is rewarded. H2 cannot be read as "claims got better".
- **H4 measures Algora, not agents.** New bounty issues the app commented on rose to 171 a month in H2 2024, then fell: 156 (H1 2025), 117 (H2 2025), 49 (H1 2026). Then they stopped: 219 in April-May 2026, 2 in June, and 0 from July to October. A stop that abrupt matches Algora winding down its public bounty board. We cannot separate this from the agent era.
